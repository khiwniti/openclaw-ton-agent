# Incident Report: INC-2026-08-20-001

**Date**: 2026-08-20  
**Severity**: High  
**Status**: Resolved & Mitigated in Production  
**Affected Subsystems**: `packages/exit-manager`, `packages/executor`, `packages/scanner`, `packages/risk-gates`, On-Chain Trading Loop  

---

## 1. Trigger & Summary
On production Fly.io deployment (`openclaw-ton-agent`), automated position closing logic was failing to exit positions after the 30-minute time-stop limit had passed. Positions were remaining in an unclosed "holding" state for $>200\text{ minutes}$. In addition, new buy orders were experiencing batch-blocking and balance lockouts.

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis

1. **Time-Stop Precedence & Undefined Handling (`decide.ts`)**:
   - `time_stop` check was placed at the bottom of the exit precedence chain.
   - Deserialized `pos.timeStopMs = undefined` evaluated `now - pos.entryTs >= undefined` to `false` in JavaScript, permanently bypassing the time-stop check.

2. **Overly Wide ATR Reversal Multiplier**:
   - `chandelierMultiplier` was hardcoded to `2.5x ATR` ($\approx 12.5\%$), which meant momentum reversals never triggered before reaching the full $5\%$ stop-loss.

3. **Closed Position Resurrection on Container Boot (`continuous.ts`)**:
   - Closed positions from `positions-mainnet.ndjson` were being resurrected as open by Step 2 in `orders-mainnet.ndjson`, filling the open position limit (19 positions) and blocking new trades.

4. **Sequential Batch Blocking**:
   - The executor processed orders sequentially in a blocking loop, causing transactions to wait $20\text{s}$ per position for on-chain seqno increments.

---

## 3. Mitigations Applied

1. **Priority 1 Hard Time-Stop**:
   - Moved `effectiveTimeStopMs` check to the top of `stepPosition()` with null/undefined safety and a default $30\text{m}$ limit for snipe mode.
2. **Two-Tier Momentum Fade Protection**:
   - Tightened `mode: "snipe"` ATR trailing to `1.2x ATR`.
   - Added automatic profit-lock exit when price gives back $50\%\text{--}65\%$ from its peak gain.
3. **Asynchronous Priority Order Queue Manager (`OrderQueueManager`)**:
   - HIGH priority for exits (TP, SL, time-stop, momentum fade).
   - NORMAL priority for buys, dispatched continuously without batch waiting.
   - Non-blocking concurrent price fetches across all open positions.
4. **Multi-DEX & Multi-Hop USDT Routing**:
   - Automatically probes and routes across STON.fi, DeDust V2, and USDT multi-hop pools.
5. **Closed Position Journal Protection**:
   - Tracked `closedTokens` set to prevent closed positions from being resurrected on restart.
6. **Fast Scanner & Cooldowns**:
   - Scanner interval: $10\text{s}$, Seen TTL: $15\text{s}$, Risk Gates cooldown: $15\text{s}$, Min Order: $0.15\text{ TON}$.

---

- **Test Suites**: All 10 workspaces passing (100% green).
- **On-Chain Proof**: Verified automated $30\text{m}$ time-stop exits and TON fund returns on Tonscan.
- **Production Status**: Deployed and actively trading on Fly.io.
